The Found Money approach

Before you chase the next shiny marketing tactic, let's monetize what you've already built.

We call it found money because that's exactly what it is: revenue that already exists inside the relationships, audience, and channels you have. It has simply never been unlocked.

01

Relationships

Money sitting in relationships you already have.

A buyers list that has never been re-engaged.
Past clients ready to send business your way if you give them a reason and a way to do it.
An email list treated like a broadcast channel instead of a relationship channel.
02

Attention

Money sitting in an audience who is watching, but hasn't taken the next step.

Followers, subscribers, and leads who are watching but not buying, because the trust and positioning aren't there yet.
Prospects who went quiet after an initial conversation, simply because you didn't have an effective follow-up strategy.
03

Untapped

Money sitting in channels you have access to but haven't put to work.

A LinkedIn network full of exactly the right people, with no strategy behind it.
Channels you have never deployed, tested, or capitalized on.
A sales process designed to speak to everyone, and as a result, connects with no one.
